Arguments (optional): `$ARGUMENTS` — topic, e.g. `/research boho runner rugs US`.

1. Detect mode; connected → pick the shop (ask once if several) for the fit check.
2. Delegate to **market-research-agent** with: topic, market (default US), shop profile path, time window (next 90 days).
3. Present: opportunity table, price bands, seasonality window, gaps, fit verdict.
4. Offer next in one line: `/new-product` (original designs from this) or `/seo` (keywords).
5. Connected: save the report to `data/reports/YYYY-MM-DD_<shop>_research.md`.
