## Construction — what can honestly be claimed

| Construction | What it is | Typical pile | Selling points | Never say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hand tufted | Yarn punched into canvas with a tufting gun, latex + cotton cloth backing | 0.4–0.75 in (10–19 mm), cut/loop/high-low | plush, dense, custom shapes & designs easy, soft underfoot | "hand knotted", "reversible" |
| Hand knotted | Individual knots on a loom, fringe is part of the rug | 0.25–0.6 in | heirloom, decades of life, reversible-look back | — |
| Flatweave / Kilim | Woven on loom, no pile | flat, ~0.2 in (5 mm) | reversible, lightweight, easy clean, under-door clearance | "plush", "thick pile" |
| Dhurrie | Cotton (or wool) flatweave, Indian handloom | flat | reversible, washable (cotton, small sizes), budget-friendly, summer rug | "tufted" |
| Handloom / hand woven | Loom-woven pile or loop | low 0.25–0.4 in | durable, textured | — |
| Jute / braided | Braided or woven natural fibre | 0.3–0.5 in | natural, eco, texture, layering base | "soft plush", "washable" |
| Block print (dhurrie/cotton) | Hand block printed cotton flatweave | flat | artisanal print, lightweight | — |

Use "handmade" for all. Use "hand tufted / hand knotted / handwoven" precisely.

## Materials

| Material | Words to use | Care line | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wool | soft, naturally stain-resistant, warm, durable | Vacuum without beater bar; blot spills; professional clean | New wool rugs **shed** for a few weeks — always say so (prevents bad reviews) |
| Cotton | lightweight, breathable, casual | Small sizes gentle machine wash cold (only if owner confirms); else spot clean | |
| Jute | natural, earthy, textured, eco | Dry clean / shake out; keep away from damp | Not for bathrooms |
| Wool-jute blend | texture + softness | Vacuum, spot clean | |
| Wool-cotton blend | soft face, strong base | Vacuum, spot clean | State blend % if known |
| Viscose / bamboo silk accents | sheen, highlights | Dry clean only, avoid water | Must be disclosed |

Always recommend a rug pad for flatweaves and small rugs (slip safety).

## Shipping facts to keep handy (fill real values in shop profile)

- Rugs ship rolled (large) or folded (small/flatweave) in waterproof wrap. Fold creases relax in 1–2 weeks — say so.
- Courier charges on **volumetric weight** (L×W×H cm ÷ 5000). Tufted wool ≈ 0.45–0.6 lb per sq ft (2.2–3 kg/m²); flatweave ≈ 0.25–0.35 lb per sq ft.
- Typical made-to-order lead time: tufted 2–3 weeks, knotted 6–12 weeks, flatweave 1–3 weeks. Use the shop profile's real numbers.
